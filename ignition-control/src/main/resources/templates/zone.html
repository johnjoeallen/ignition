Members

These are the people who can open this team's console. An admin can also manage the members listed below. A member can do everything else here, including creating apps and cutting releases.

To add someone, they must already have an Ignition account. Ask them to sign up first, or have a platform admin invite them. Adding them here also creates their Forgejo login, so there is no separate git step. Their git username is their email address in a form Forgejo accepts, shown in the username column.

Your git username, password and personal access token are shown above and on each app's page, next to its clone URL. They're visible only to you — not even team admins or platform admins can see them. If you lose your password or token, use the regenerate button next to it to get a new one. The old one stops working immediately.
